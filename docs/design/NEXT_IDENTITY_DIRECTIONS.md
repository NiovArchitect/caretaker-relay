# Next Identity Directions — Caretaker Relay

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Status:** Design Division directions for founder/product selection  

## Direction A — Deep Care Intelligence
- **Core:** Near-black medical ink `#0a1c24`, luminous signal cyan-teal, pale clinical surfaces  
- **Emotion:** Precision, late-night reliability, AI calm  
- **Risk:** Can feel cold for family caregivers  

## Direction B — Future Home Care
- **Core:** Warm stone neutrals, soft sage + blue intelligence accents  
- **Emotion:** Human, domestic, reassuring  
- **Risk:** Less “premium future product” memorability for judges  

## Direction C — Precision Relay (SELECTED for product CSS)
- **Core:** Deep ink `#0b2430`, restrained signal teal `#0c5c6a` / `#1f8a9a`, warm amber vitality `#c9893a`  
- **Emotion:** Continuity, handoff, protected center, trustworthy  
- **Depth:** Tonal gradients + borders + soft shadows — **no scroll blur**  
- **Logo:** Two arcs + protected center (relay loop)  

## Selection rationale
- Brand distinction without neon cyberpunk  
- Caregiver warmth via amber + neutrals  
- Physician trust via deep ink and restrained accent  
- Performance-safe (static stage gradients only)  
- Aligns with “relay / handoff / protected person” meaning
