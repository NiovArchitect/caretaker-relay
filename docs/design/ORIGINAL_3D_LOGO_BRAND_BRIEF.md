# Caretaker Relay — Original 3D Logo Brand Brief

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Status:** Internal creative brief for original identity (no prior mark reuse)  
**Temporary live identity:** Wordmark-only until founder selects a concept  

## Brand promise

One trusted care continuum: the right people keep a shared, verified picture of care as it moves across people, time, and handoffs — without losing the person at the center.

## Brand attributes

| Attribute | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Continuity | Nothing critical is lost between shifts or people |
| Relay | Context moves to the right authorized person |
| Human center | Dignity and agency of the care recipient |
| Ambient intelligence | Quiet organization; never noisy chatbot spectacle |
| Trust | Epistemic honesty (reported / confirmed / uncertain) |
| Collaboration | One operating flow, not fragmented tools |

## Visual personality

Premium product design meets calm medical intelligence: sculpted, precise, restrained depth — closer to refined medical devices and spatial computing than hospital seals or neon AI.

## Audience

Family caregivers, DSPs, clinicians, care coordinators, care recipients, competition judges.

## Emotional response

Calm confidence. “This will hold.” Not urgency, not cuteness, not surveillance.

## Competitive distinction

Not a portal, not staffing software, not a chatbot. Ambient continuity across a care circle.

## Prohibited visual territory

Hands, person-in-circle, ring-and-dot, pin silhouette, hearts, crosses, shields, chatbot sparkles, generic neural nets, orbits, infinity clichés, insurance seals, staffing badges, crypto chrome, esports neon, reuse of rejected Option A geometry.

## 3D material language

Satin ceramic, soft anodized metal, mineral glass. Controlled highlights only. No chrome circus.

## Color strategy

Deep ocean ink `#0B2430`, mineral teal `#0C5C6A` / `#1F8A9A`, optional champagne accent `#C4A574` sparingly. Monochrome must succeed alone.

## Wordmark strategy

Custom spacing of “Caretaker Relay”; stable weight; “Relay” may use restrained teal but must feel one brand.

## Favicon strategy

Independent micro geometry of the selected concept — never a scaled hero, never prior ring-dot.

## Review approval (process)

Brand Guardian · UI Designer · UX Researcher · Healthcare · Reality Checker: brief territory accepted for original concept exploration only — **not** logo approval.
