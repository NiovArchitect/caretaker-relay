# Original Logo Differentiation Review

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** Internal screening only — **not** formal trademark clearance.

## Screening categories

Compared concept silhouettes against common patterns in:

- healthcare / caregiver apps  
- insurance brands  
- AI assistants  
- collaboration SaaS  
- staffing platforms  
- patient portals  
- medical-device marks  

## Findings

| Concept | Resemblance risk | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| 1 Continuity Object | Low–medium | Continuous band forms exist in logistics; differentiated by bevel + single transfer volume |
| 2 Trusted Interlock | Medium | Interlock marks common in security SaaS; protected void + soft ceramic reading differentiates |
| 3 Care Signal | Medium | Risk of Wi-Fi/pulse cliché — mitigated by closed conduit + single elongated node (not bars) |
| 4 Coherent Handoff | Low | Precision join is less common; avoid looking like a broken equals sign |

## Honest limitation

This is design-team visual screening, not a legal search. Founder should still run trademark counsel before broad commercial lock-in.

## Prohibited near-misses avoided

No hands, person icons, medical crosses, chatbot bubbles, neural nodes, infinity loops, location pins, Option A ring-center.
