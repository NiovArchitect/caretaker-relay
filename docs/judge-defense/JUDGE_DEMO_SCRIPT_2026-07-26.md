## Aha journey (≈12 minutes)

### AHA 1 — Five-second orientation (Marcus)

1. Sign in as Marcus.  
2. Land on **Today**.  
3. Point to six-cell scan: Who / Needs you / Changed / Already handled / Coming up / Who is helping.  
4. Point to **What Relay watches for you** (ambient explanation).  

### AHA 2 — One sentence → coordinated care

1. **Ask or update Relay**.  
2. Type: `Evelyn seemed more tired after lunch and only finished half her sandwich.`  
3. Show verify / confirm path (or fixture understand).  
4. Return to Today — changed / notifications path.  
5. Emphasize: reported status, who, when; no silent truth.

### AHA 3 — Ambient continuity (incoming)

1. In Relay ask: `Catch me up on what happened overnight.`  
2. Show timed changes + still open + next steps.  

### AHA 4 — Safe medication boundary

1. Ask about unclear dose / Protocol 9-Delta.  
2. Show refusal or open verification — **never invents dose**.  
3. Show safe next steps + Care resolve path.

### AHA 5 — Collaboration

1. **People** → Message next to Maya (or Daniel).  
2. Coordination opens with correct person selected.  

### AHA 6 — Physician signal

1. Sign out → Dr Shah.  
2. Today **Clinic signal** card: open verification + timed changes.  
3. Care: plan vs reported admin distinction.

### AHA 7 — Privacy

1. People → **Privacy, dignity, and access** card.  
2. Role limits on member detail.  
3. Documents export is review-before-share.

### AHA 8 — Emergency readiness (bounded)

1. Today → **Essential / emergency info**.  
2. Care emergency card: disclaimer (call 911), contacts, allergies, no AI emergency dispatch.

### AHA 9 — Correction without erase

1. Confirm path then **Correct** if available in demo state.  
2. State: prior evidence retained; current truth updates.

### AHA 10 — Useful care document

1. Documents → Prepare care summary.  
2. Wait for generate (cold start may be slow).  
3. Show Action needed, human times, no raw `…749Z`.
