# Family Caregiver Recruitment Screener

**Status:** Template only — **do not send / do not recruit under automated prep**  
**Primary goal:** Confirm unpaid family/friend caregiving support in a home/community setting.

## Opening (when a human uses this)

We are preparing formative product research for a care-coordination prototype. This screener checks whether someone is a fit for a 45–60 minute session using **made-up** care scenarios.

## Inclusion (recommended)

Person currently or recently:

- Provides **unpaid** care or substantial support for someone in a **home or community** setting, **and**  
- Helps with coordination of daily care information (e.g. routines, appointments, medications, updates among people), **or**  
- Is a primary family/friend organizer of care updates.

## Exclusion (recommended)

- Seeking medical advice from the research team  
- Only interested in employment/workforce management tools  
- Unable or unwilling to use a synthetic (not real-PHI) scenario  
- Under age threshold set by founder/organization policy  

## Questions (minimal — no diagnosis required)

1. Do you currently (or in the past year) help care for or coordinate care for a family member or friend at home or in the community?  
   - Yes / No  

2. About how often do you help?  
   - Daily / Several times a week / Weekly / Less often / Prefer not to say  

3. Do other people also help this person?  
   - Yes / No / Prefer not to say  

4. Do you ever help with any of: medications reminders/tracking, appointments, daily routines, or sharing updates with others?  
   - Yes (which, optional) / No / Prefer not to say  

5. How do you usually keep track of care information today? (open; e.g. texts, calls, notes, memory, portal, calendar)  

6. How comfortable are you trying a simple computer or phone prototype?  
   - Comfortable / Somewhat / Not very / Prefer not to say  

## Do NOT ask

- Specific diagnoses of the care recipient  
- Real medication lists  
- Insurance details  
- Government IDs  

## Outcome

- **Eligible for invite** / **Not eligible** / **Hold**  
- Assign code only after scheduled: CG-00N (in private log)

## Diversity (optional notes — do not require)

Support type, multi-helper household, meds/appointments coordination, long-distance, tech comfort — only if offered or lightly answered above.
