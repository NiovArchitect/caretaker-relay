# First Caregiver Research Cycle

**Objective:** Test whether Caretaker Relay reduces cognitive/coordination burden for real caregivers, and whether they understand, trust, and can control the AI — on the **frozen Judge Loop build**.

**Hypothesis under test (founder, not validated):**  
One natural caregiver update can become a shared, verified, permission-aware care picture that reduces re-explaining and holds dangerous ambiguity for human judgment.

**Status:** Protocol ready. **Sessions conducted: 0.**

## Core questions

1. Does Caretaker Relay reduce the cognitive and coordination burden caregivers actually experience?  
2. Do real caregivers understand, trust, and know how to control the AI?

Not a feature-preference survey.

## Participant plan (recommended)

### Primary (required for cycle success)

| Class | N | Notes |
