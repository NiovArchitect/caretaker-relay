# Moderator Guide — First Caregiver Research Cycle

**Build under test:** frozen Judge Loop — see `RESEARCH_BUILD_MANIFEST.md`  
**Audience:** Human moderator (not an automated agent running Session 1)

## Role

You facilitate. You do **not** sell. You do **not** lead participants to praise the AI.

## Before the session

1. Confirm frozen app/foundation SHAs match the manifest.  
2. Boot stack with **synthetic** Olivia scenario only.  
3. Clear or reset lab state if prior demos polluted data.  
4. Prepare observation sheet + timer.  
5. Consent: voluntary, withdraw anytime, synthetic data only.  
6. Disable recording unless explicit permission.

## Opening script (non-leading)

> “We’re testing a prototype that helps people coordinate care for someone at home. You’ll use **made-up** care information about a person named Olivia — not your real records. There are no right answers. I’m interested in what makes sense, what confuses you, and how this compares to what you already do.”

## Bias controls — PROHIBITED leading lines

**Do not say:**

- “Wasn’t that easier?”  
- “Did you like how the AI caught that?”  
- “Wouldn’t this save you time?”  
- “Isn’t that safer?”  
- “Most people love this part.”

**Prefer:**

- “What happened there?”  
- “What did you expect?”  
- “How would you handle that today?”  
- “What was confusing?”  
- “What, if anything, was useful?”  
- “What would you change?”  
- “What would make you distrust this?”

## Behavior before opinion

For each task:

1. **Observe first** (no explanation of the screen).  
2. Note clicks, ignores, pauses, misreads, spontaneous talk.  
3. Then ask questions.  

Preserve **observed behavior** vs **stated preference** as separate evidence types.

## PHI / safety stop conditions

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Participant starts entering real PHI into the prototype | Gently redirect to synthetic Olivia scenario; do not encourage real data in lab |
| Emotional distress | Pause, offer break/stop; do not continue to “finish the protocol” |
| Medical advice seeking | “I can’t give medical advice. For dosing or symptoms, talk to a clinician.” Do not use Relay as clinical authority |
| Request for real-system promises | Be honest about prototype limits |

## Synthetic-only rule

Do **not** ask participants to put real medication lists, diagnoses, or identifiers into the app.

## After session

1. Complete observation sheet same day.  
2. Log findings in `FINDINGS_REGISTER.md` with honest tags.  
3. No product change until decision log entry exists (except session-blocking P0 with amendment to manifest).  
4. Never invent quotes you did not capture with consent.
