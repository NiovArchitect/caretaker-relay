# Post-Session Questions

Ask **after** tasks. Keep neutral. Skip any that already surfaced clearly.

## Understanding

1. In your own words, what is Caretaker Relay for?  
2. Who is it mainly helping?  
3. What did “Needs your attention” mean to you?

## Value

4. What, if anything, was useful?  
5. What felt unnecessary?  
6. What is missing for your real life?

## Trust and AI

7. When did you feel most confident about what Relay showed?  
8. When did you feel least confident?  
9. What would make you distrust it?

## Safety

10. What did you think when Relay asked you to check the medication information?  
11. Did you ever expect Relay to tell you what dose to give? (Do not encourage that expectation.)

## Continuity

12. How do you hand off care information today?  
13. Would the handoff you saw change that? How?

## Privacy / dignity

14. What should the care recipient control?  
15. What should never be shared automatically?

## Burden

16. Compared with your current process, was this more work, less work, or different work?  
17. What would have to be true for this to free time with the person you care for?

## Closing

18. If we could change one thing before you used this again, what would it be?  
19. Is there anything we didn’t ask that we should know?

**Do not** ask: “Would you recommend this?” as a success metric in formative cycle 1 unless naturally volunteered.
