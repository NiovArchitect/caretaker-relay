# Session Tasks — First Cycle

**No coaching on UI before Task 1.**  
**Synthetic Olivia scenario throughout.**

---

## TASK 1 — First five seconds

**Setup:** Open **Today**. Do not explain.

**Prompt:**

- “What do you think this is showing you?”  
- “Who do you think it’s for?”  
- “What would you do first?”  
- “What do you think needs attention? What changed? What’s already handled? What’s next?”

**Capture:** comprehension of hierarchy; misinterpretations of “Needs your attention.”

---

## TASK 2 — Record a care update

**Setup:** Give a short synthetic situation (see scenario pack in `FIRST_CAREGIVER_RESEARCH_CYCLE` / demo script variants). Example seed (moderator may paraphrase, not force exact words):

> You’ve been with Olivia this morning. She seemed dizzy getting up, ate around nine, mentioned taking two of the blue pills, and Maya is coming around three instead of two. You want Maya to know what’s going on.

**Prompt:** “Tell Relay what happened — however you’d normally share an update.”

**Observe:** voice vs text; form-seeking; command language; time; action count; confusion.

**Do not** force the exact judge utterance if natural language differs — natural speech is the point.

---

## TASK 3 — Verify

**Setup:** After Relay shows interpretation.

**Prompt:**

- “What do you think Relay believes happened?”  
- “What does ‘Reported’ / ‘Needs checking’ mean to you?”  
- “Is anything missing or invented?”

**Observe:** whether multi-event grouping is clear.

---

## TASK 4 — Medication ambiguity

**Setup:** Ensure a high-consequence ambiguous med moment is visible (or guide scenario so it appears).  
**Do not** ask Relay for a dose recommendation.

**Prompt:**

- “Why do you think it stopped here?”  
- “What does it know? What doesn’t it know?”  
- “Does this make you trust it more or less?”  
- “What would you do next?”

---

## TASK 5 — Correct Relay

**Setup:** Introduce a synthetic mistake or intentional change (e.g. meal time wrong, visit time wrong).

**Prompt:** “Something’s not right — fix it the way you would if this were real.”

**Observe:** can they correct without coaching; do they understand history isn’t simply erased?

---

## TASK 6 — Handoff

**Prompt first (before showing handoff):**

- “Imagine Maya is taking over. What does she need to know?”

Then show Relay’s handoff.

**Prompt:**

- “How does this compare to what you said Maya needs?”  
- “Would this reduce re-explaining?”  
- “What’s missing or unnecessary?”

---

## TASK 7 — Privacy / control

**Prompt:** Reason about who should see what for:

- another family member  
- secondary friend caregiver  
- professional home caregiver  
- provider  
- care recipient  

**Prompt:** “What would feel invasive? How should access be removed? What should never auto-share?”

---

## TASK 8 — Overall burden comparison

**Prompt:**

- “How do you handle this kind of coordination **today** (texts, calls, notes, memory, portals, etc.)?”  
- “How many places do you usually check?”  
- “What commonly gets missed?”  
- “Compared with that, what was useful or not useful here?”  

**Do not lead** them to say Relay is better.

---

## Optional micro-prompts (anywhere, non-leading)

- “What would make you distrust this?”  
- “When would you want to see the source?”  
- “Would this free time for the person you care for — or become another thing to manage?”
