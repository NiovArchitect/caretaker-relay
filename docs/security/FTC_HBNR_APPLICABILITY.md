# FTC Health Breach Notification Rule — Applicability Note

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Source:** 16 CFR Part 318 (FTC Health Breach Notification Rule)

## Status

**PARTIAL / EXTERNAL LEGAL DETERMINATION REQUIRED**

If Caretaker Relay operates as a personal health record (PHR) vendor or related entity **outside** HIPAA coverage, FTC HBNR may require breach notification for unauthorized acquisition of unsecured PHR identifiable health information.

## Product implications

1. Do not assume HIPAA coverage eliminates FTC analysis for consumer-facing modes.  
2. Maintain vendor inventory, encryption in transit, and access logs.  
3. Incident response and notification timelines are **EXTERNAL** operational dependencies.  
4. No statement that FTC HBNR “does not apply” without counsel review of deployment model.
