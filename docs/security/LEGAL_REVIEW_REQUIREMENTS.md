# Legal Review Requirements

External counsel / compliance must review before production regulated launch:

1. Covered entity vs business associate vs consumer app classification per customer.  
2. BAA templates with customers and subprocessors (hosting, AI, email, SMS, analytics).

4. Personal representative and supported decision-making documentation.  
5. State consumer health data laws and FTC HBNR obligations.  
6. Breach notification playbooks and timelines.  
7. Marketing / judge language review (no “HIPAA compliant” without determination).
